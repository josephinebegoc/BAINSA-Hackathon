Listening mode

What does each mode sound like?
Explore
Every cell with its row, column and value: “Italy. August revenue. €62,000.”
Concise
Just the values, to move quickly: “€100,000 … €110,000 … €121,000.” Flagged cells still speak.
Sound Graph
Ordinary numbers stay silent. Press R to play the row as tones, C the column. Higher values play higher notes, and the notes move from left to right across the row, like a line chart you can hear.
