Accessible Attention for Excel

Screen readers make spreadsheet cells readable. We make visual attention accessible.

Press L for the demo sheet, or U to upload your own spreadsheet.

Keyboard shortcuts
  • Arrow keys move one cell
  • O overview of the sheet
  • Space or N next flagged cell (add Shift for previous)
  • W or ? why was this cell flagged?
  • L load the demo sheet
  • U upload your own spreadsheet
  • D describe everything about this cell
  • R / C read the whole row / column (as tones in Trend Scan)
  • 1 Explore · 2 Concise · 3 Trend Scan
  • F change which cues you hear (all, author, data, errors)
  • Esc stop speaking
  • H hear these shortcuts
